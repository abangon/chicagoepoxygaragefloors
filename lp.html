FAQ

Questions homeowners ask

How much does an epoxy garage floor cost in Chicago?
A typical 2-car garage (400–500 sq ft) runs in the range shown in the price table above, depending on the system and the condition of the slab. Cracks, old paint and moisture add cost. See the full price breakdown or use the calculator.
Can you coat a garage floor in winter?
Yes. We work year-round, including winter, in garages and basements, as long as the temperature and humidity meet what the coating requires. We check that for your floor before we schedule.
How long does the install take?
A 1-car or 2-car garage takes 1–2 days. A 3-car garage takes 2–3 days.
How long before I can park on it?
It depends on the coating. Polyaspartic cures faster than epoxy. We give you the exact times for the system you choose.
Will it peel from hot tires?
Hot-tire peeling happens when coating sits on top of smooth, unprepared concrete. That is why every floor starts with diamond grinding, which opens the surface so the coating bonds into it.
Can you fix the cracks first?
Yes. Crack repair is priced separately, per linear foot, after we see the floor. The rate is in the price table above.
Which areas do you serve?
Chicago neighborhoods and suburbs from the North Shore to the southwest suburbs and the Fox Valley. See the full list or check your ZIP above.
