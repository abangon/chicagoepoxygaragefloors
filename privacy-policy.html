What we collect

We collect information you give us (name, phone number, email, ZIP code, project details, and photos you upload), information about your calls and text messages with us, and technical data about how you use our site (such as device, browser and pages viewed). Photos may contain background details; please don't upload images of people or documents.

How we use it

To answer your request, give you an estimate, schedule and do the work, keep records of our conversations, measure which ads and pages work, prevent spam, and improve the site.

Call recording and AI analysis

Calls to the phone numbers on this site go through a call-tracking service. Calls may be recorded and transcribed, and we may use automated tools, including AI, to summarize calls and rate how we handled them. Callers can press 2 at the start of the call to talk without being recorded. We do not use voiceprints or voice recognition to identify you, and we do not collect biometric identifiers. Our service providers may use this data only to provide services to us and may not use call recordings to train their own models or for their own purposes. We keep call audio for up to 90 days and transcripts and scores for up to 12 months, unless needed to resolve a dispute.

Text messages

If you check the consent box on our form, we may contact you by text message about your request. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time; reply HELP for help. We do not share your mobile number with third parties for their own marketing.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand site traffic. It uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages visited and device data. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on. We do not send your name, email or phone number to Google Analytics. Event data is kept for 14 months.

Session replay and heatmaps (Microsoft Clarity)

With your permission (the Accept button in our cookie notice), we use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use and interact with the site through behavioral metrics, heatmaps and session replay, so we can fix problems and improve the site. Form fields and the text you type are masked and are not recorded. Session recordings are kept by Microsoft for 30 days. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for how Microsoft collects and uses data.

Advertising and conversion measurement (Google Ads)

When you contact us after clicking an ad, we may send Google a click identifier and, if you submit a request, hashed (scrambled) versions of your email address and phone number, plus whether the request turned into a job, so we can tell which ads work. Google uses this data under its customer data terms and does not share it with other advertisers.

Sharing

We share your information with service providers who help us run the business and the site (hosting, call tracking, messaging, analytics). We do not sell your information. We honor Global Privacy Control signals.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing dgroup.illinois@gmail.com. We respond within 45 days and will not treat you differently for asking.

Do Not Track

Our site does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, but we honor Global Privacy Control. Other parties, such as Google and Microsoft, may collect data about your activity across sites when you use our site.

Security and retention

We use reasonable security measures to protect your information. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires. We keep lead information for up to 24 months, consent records for at least 4 years, and call audio for up to 90 days, unless a longer period is needed for legal reasons.

Children

This site is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact
